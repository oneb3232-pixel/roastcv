Yes. Your resume text is sent directly to the AI for analysis and is never stored, logged, or shared with anyone. We don't have a database of resumes. Each session is completely private.

No account needed for the free tier. Just open the app and start. For paid plans, you'll go through a simple Razorpay checkout — no password required.

Currently: Software Engineer, Product Manager, Data Scientist, Designer, and Marketing. More roles are being added. The AI gives role-specific feedback tailored to what recruiters in that field actually look for.
Will this work for freshers (no experience)?
Absolutely — in fact, freshers benefit the most. The AI knows how to highlight projects, internships, and academic achievements in a way that stands out even without work experience.

ChatGPT is a general tool — you'd need to write the right prompts yourself. Resume Roaster is purpose-built: it gives structured scores, role-specific criticism, ATS analysis, and a formatted rewrite automatically. No prompt engineering needed.
